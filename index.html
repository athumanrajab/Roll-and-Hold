A quick guide

How to play

Be the first player to reach 100 points. Take turns rolling the die and decide when it is safe to bank your points.

  1. Roll to add the die value to your current score. Roll again to build it up.
  2. Roll a 1 and you lose all unbanked points for that turn. Your turn ends.
  3. Hold to add your current score to your total. Your turn then passes to the other player.
  4. Reach 100 points in your total score to win. Choose New Game to start over.
